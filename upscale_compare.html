What to look for

  • faces, elder: skin turning waxy or plastic, eyes and hair gaining detail that wasn't there.
  • skyline, rubble: play at 1× and watch for shimmer or crawling texture. These models work one frame at a time.
  • e-waste: a photograph full of small objects. Watch for shapes the model makes up.
  • Judge at the size you actually watch. 2× and 4× show what changed, not what you'll notice from the couch.

Speed on this Mac

Model time per frame on the five clips, including GPU transfers. The film is 124,300 frames. These runs are short, and a fanless MacBook Air slows down over hours of sustained load, so expect longer than this for a whole film.
